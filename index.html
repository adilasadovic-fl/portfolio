About

I'm a product designer who codes — I design directly in the same tools and repositories engineering ships from, so the artifact under review is the real thing, not a static stand-in for it.

My work lately has focused on the structural side of design: how a prototype stays a single source of truth as it moves from idea to production, how feedback stays attached to the thing it's about, and how design systems get consumed as code instead of screenshots. I care more about whether the system holds together than whether the deck looks good — architecture over effects.
